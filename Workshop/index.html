01

Make a study plan that sticks

Choose one goal, break it into small tasks, and schedule short breaks. A simple plan can make a big assignment feel manageable.

  • Start with the task that matters most.
  • Work in focused blocks, then take a short break.
  • At the end, note what you will do next.
